# MicroAI Studios engineering

We are building governed AI workflow software: systems that can explain what they intend to do, obtain the right human authority, execute through a narrow control point, and leave useful evidence.

> **Current status: consolidation and security hardening.** The organization is reducing a large prototype portfolio to a small number of canonical private products and independently useful public contracts. A repository, screenshot, workflow, or historical deployment claim is not a production guarantee.

## What we are focused on

Our current 90-day product focus is deliberately narrow:

- a private LaunchOpsPro operating path for founders and lean teams;
- a ProofGuard-powered governance-readiness offer and contract model; and
- the canonical AI-integration course.

Founder Edition is a private, loopback-only founder dogfood/content-capture environment—not the public SaaS. Other verticals and experiments are frozen, capped, migrated, returned to a client/personal account, or retired unless they directly prove one of those three paths.

Product and repository names remain working names until appropriate clearance or replacement. Private product repositories are not linked from this public profile.

## Open-source direction

We prefer two excellent public projects over dozens of half-supported repositories. The proposed first flagships are:

- `proofguard-spec`: governance/evidence schemas, conformance tests, synthetic fixtures, and safe tooling; and
- `launchops-core`: versioned job/artifact/approval contracts, SDKs, a side-effect-free simulator, and compatibility tests.

They are **not public releases yet**. Each must first pass provenance and license review, a private secret/history scan, an independent ten-minute quickstart, green CI, a working private security-reporting route, documented limitations, and an explicit owner publication decision. Production engines, billing/tenant state, prompts, provider adapters, vertical workflow logic, customer data, and paid curriculum remain private.

## Engineering standard

Every supported public repository must provide:

1. the problem and intended user;
2. an architecture and explicit trust boundaries;
3. a setup path that reaches first success in under ten minutes;
4. lint, typecheck, test, and build commands reproduced by CI;
5. a security model and private vulnerability-reporting route;
6. current limitations and unsupported uses;
7. path-level license/provenance clarity; and
8. evidence labels at the point of every demo, screenshot, metric, fixture, or claim.

Consequential automation must fail closed. A schema-valid message conveys no identity, tenant ownership, billing entitlement, human approval, governance verdict, callback trust, or execution authority. Those decisions belong to an authenticated server-side boundary and must be bound to the exact actor, action, object, policy version, and time window.

## Evidence vocabulary

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Live** | Independently rechecked against a named deployment, version, and commit. |
| **Recorded** | Captured from a named prior run; it may not match the current commit. |
| **Synthetic** | Generated test data containing no real customer, founder, learner, payment, health, or credential material. |
| **Illustrative** | A design, mockup, example, or target state—not implementation proof. |
| **Unverified** | Present or claimed but not independently reproduced during the current review. |

If a claim is not labeled and tied to reproducible evidence, treat it as unverified.

## Repository lifecycle

We do not bulk-copy repositories, erase attribution, or delete branches to improve a dashboard. Before a source is archived, we inventory its default and every unmerged branch, classify unique work and rights, preserve accepted work through an ordinary reviewed pull request, test the canonical destination, privately review secret history, add a final source pointer, and obtain repository-specific owner approval. Archive is the default retirement action; deletion is exceptional and irreversible.

See the organization’s [portfolio contract](https://github.com/MicroAIStudios-DAO/.github/blob/main/docs/PORTFOLIO.md) and [binding consolidation gate](https://github.com/MicroAIStudios-DAO/.github/blob/main/docs/CONSOLIDATION.md). These documents describe decisions and gates; they are not evidence that every migration has occurred.

## Security

Do not open a public issue containing a suspected vulnerability, credential, private endpoint, customer data, or unredacted log. Follow the [organization security policy](https://github.com/MicroAIStudios-DAO/.github/security/policy) and use GitHub private vulnerability reporting on the affected public repository when available.

## Contributing and support

Until a flagship reaches its public-release gate, the public organization is documentation-first and offers no production support SLA. When releases begin, contributions will enter through scoped issues and reviewed pull requests; sponsorship will fund maintenance, fixtures, documentation, and public-good compatibility work—not certification, roadmap control, exclusivity, or commercial support.

Compliance mappings are engineering aids, not certifications. Licensing, legal, security, and financial statements are analysis rather than professional advice.
